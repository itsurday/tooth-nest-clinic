Using the logo

Do

  • Leave clear space around it of at least the height of the tooth's smile.
  • Keep the full logo at least 96 px (2.5 cm in print) wide; use the mark below that.
  • Use the white version on dark green, charcoal or photos.
  • Scale it evenly (keep the proportions).

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate or redraw it.
  • Change the colours or add shadows, outlines or glows.
  • Put the full-colour logo on busy photos or mid-tone backgrounds.
  • Use the old PNG with the white box on coloured backgrounds.

How we sound

Warm, plain, reassuring

  • Short sentences and everyday words: "tooth-coloured filling", not "composite restoration".
  • Explain what will happen and how it will feel.
  • Speak to nervous patients kindly; never scare people into treatment.

Honest and factual

  • No "best dentist in Hyderabad", guaranteed results or pushy discounts (Dental Council of India ethics rules).
  • Show qualifications and registration numbers.
  • Real photos of the real clinic only.
