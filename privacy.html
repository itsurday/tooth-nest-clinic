Privacy notice

How we look after your personal data. Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Tooth Nest Dental Clinic ("we", "us"), Manjira Nagar Colony, Alwal, Secunderabad, Telangana 500010, is responsible for the personal data described here. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the rules made under it.

What this website collects

  • Nothing by default. This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or social media pixels, and you can read every page without giving us any information.
  • Phone and WhatsApp. If you call or message us, we receive your phone number, your name if you give it, and what you tell us.
  • Google Maps. The map on our contact page loads from Google only if you click "Show map". Google's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Server logs. Our website host keeps short-term technical logs (such as IP address and pages requested) to keep the site secure and working.

What the clinic collects when you visit

To treat you safely we keep a dental record: your name, contact details, age, medical history, medicines, allergies, examination findings, X-rays, treatment notes and payments. Health information is sensitive, and we treat it with extra care.

Why we use it

  • To book, confirm and remind you of appointments.
  • To examine and treat you safely.
  • To issue bills and receipts.
  • To meet legal duties, such as keeping clinical records and responding to lawful requests.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. We only send you reminders or messages you agreed to receive.

Your consent

Where we rely on your consent, we ask clearly and for a specific purpose. You can withdraw consent at any time by telling us; this does not affect anything done before you withdrew it, or records we must keep by law.

Who we share it with

  • Dental laboratories that make crowns, bridges, dentures or aligners for you (only what they need).
  • Other doctors or specialists, when needed for your care and with your agreement.
  • Service providers who help us run the clinic and this website, under contracts that protect your data.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Clinical records are kept for as long as dental and medical rules require, and then deleted or destroyed securely. Booking enquiries that do not lead to a visit are deleted within 12 months.

How we protect it

Paper records are kept in locked storage and digital records in access-controlled accounts used only by clinic staff. Please do not send X-rays, reports or photos of your mouth on WhatsApp unless we ask; bring them to the clinic instead.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or complete it;
  • erase it, where we are not required to keep it;
  • name someone to exercise your rights if you are unable to.

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

For patients under 18, we collect and use personal data with the consent of a parent or guardian.

Contact us about privacy

Speak to us at the clinic, call +91 97047 37577, or write to Tooth Nest Dental Clinic, Manjira Nagar Colony, Alwal, Secunderabad, Telangana 500010. We will reply within 30 days.
