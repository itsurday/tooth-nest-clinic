Your visit

What happens, step by step

  1. Book

    Call, WhatsApp or book online. Tell us if you are in pain.

  2. Welcome

    A short form about your health, medicines and allergies.

  3. Examination

    Dr. Ramya checks your teeth and gums, with an X-ray if needed.

  4. Your plan

    We explain the options and costs. You decide, with no pressure.

  5. Treatment

    Comfortable care, with breaks whenever you need them.

  6. Follow-up

    Aftercare advice and a reminder for your next check-up.
