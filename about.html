How we work

Gentle, honest, unhurried

You are in control

No surprises

A written plan and estimate before treatment, with simpler options where they exist.

Prevention first

Your safety

Sterilisation and hygiene

  • Instruments are cleaned and steam-sterilised in an autoclave, and kept in sealed pouches until your appointment.
  • Gloves, masks, needles, suction tips and cups are single-use.
  • The chair and every surface are disinfected between patients.
  • We follow the infection-control guidance of the Dental Council of India.

To help keep the clinic clean, we ask visitors to leave footwear outside and to wear a face mask in the waiting area.
