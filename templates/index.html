Design Round 2

Rebuilt with the added design skills: Taste Skill (directions dealt from 247 studied sites, checked in a real browser at 1440, 768 and 390 px), Emil Kowalski's polish and motion rules, and Impeccable's design guidance. Every page has one live thing: a visit planner that shows the real hours for the day you pick and writes your WhatsApp message.
